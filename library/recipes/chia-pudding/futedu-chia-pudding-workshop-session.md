# FUTEDU Nutrition Workshop — Session Master Document
## Chia Pudding: Tomorrow's Breakfast, Made Today (3 flavours)

**Location:** Residencia Galileo Galilei — kitchen
**Group:** 3 tables of 5–10 players (up to 30), one flavour per table
**Recipe covered:** *Chia Pudding* in three flavours: cocoa & banana, berries & vanilla, mango & coconut
**Session length:** 60 min

---

## 1. Session Dynamic

- Each table makes **one flavour** in **individual disposable containers with lids** (one per player, up to 30 in total). No bowls, no blender, no oven: the fruit is mashed with a fork **inside the container**, then the chia and the soy drink go in on top.
- Per player: mash fruit → add chia and soy drink → stir 1 min → wait 10 min → stir again → lid on, name and flavour on the label → fridge.
- Measuring without scales: a level **2 tbsp** for the chia (≈ 25 g) and **¾ cup** (≈ 180 ml) for the soy drink. Put one measuring cup and one set of measuring spoons on each table.
- The 10-minute wait is used for the activity (**Breakfast Match**, Section 4).
- Containers stay in the fridge overnight and are picked up the next morning; they keep up to 3 days.

### Run of show (60 min)

| Min | What happens | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | **Welcome and hook.** Ask: "What did you eat this morning, and could you make it the night before?" Show the cup of chia you soaked 30 min earlier next to a spoonful of dry seeds, so they see the gel. | Facilitator |
| 5–10 | **Demo of one container**: mash the fruit, 2 tbsp chia, ¾ cup soy drink, stir. Say the three rules out loud: *measure, stir for a full minute, stir again after 10 minutes*. Assign each table its flavour. | Facilitator |
| 10–22 | **Everyone makes their pudding** (12 min). Facilitator walks the tables checking amounts. | Players |
| 22–32 | **Breakfast Match** during the first 10-minute wait (Section 4). One stir at minute 32. | Tables |
| 32–38 | **Share-back**: each table presents one situation (see key). | Tables |
| 38–45 | Second stir, lids, labels (name + flavour), into the fridge. | Players |
| 45–55 | Clean-up of tables and utensils. | Everyone |
| 55–60 | **Wrap-up line** and tomorrow's instructions: where to pick up, eat within 3 days, add toppings. | Facilitator |

**Before the players arrive (~20 min):** thaw the fruit; set out for each table the containers, spoons, forks, a measuring cup and spoons, the chia and the soy drink; soak your demo cup of chia 30 min before the session; clear fridge space; lay out the markers and labels; print one Breakfast Match sheet per table.

**After the session:** check the fridge, and next morning remind the group (message) that the puddings are ready.

---

## 2. What This Recipe Brings & How It Helps Performance

Chia seeds absorb many times their weight in liquid and turn into a gel, which is why the pudding sets overnight with no cooking. They bring **fibre and plant omega-3 fats**; with the soy drink the pudding also adds some plant protein and, if the drink is fortified, calcium. A portion is around 230 kcal.

**Framing for the group:** a **breakfast made the day before**, so it removes the "no time in the morning" excuse. The key point: chia is *not* a quick-carb food. It is light on fast energy, so the pudding is a base you **adapt to the day**: banana, oats, mango or dates when you need carbs; yogurt or peanut butter when you need protein. Contrast with Session 1 (overnight oats), where the oats were the carb source: here the carbs have to come from what you add.

**Talking point:** ask "if you trained at 7:00 tomorrow with 20 minutes to eat, would you eat this as it is?" Most will say yes; steer them to what is missing.

**Honest notes:** keep the claims modest. Chia is a good source of fibre and plant omega-3 (ALA); it does not replace oily fish or a full meal. Always soak the seeds: do not eat dry chia by the spoonful.

## 3. Shopping List — 30 people max (3 tables × 10 portions)

Per portion: 2 tbsp (25 g) chia + ¾ cup (180 ml) unsweetened soy drink + flavour.

| Ingredient | Per portion | Total (30) | Buy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chia seeds | 2 tbsp (25 g) | ~750 g | 1 kg (or 2 × 500 g) |
| Unsweetened soy drink (calcium-fortified if possible) | ¾ cup (180 ml) | ~5.4 L | 6 × 1 L |
| **Cocoa & banana table:** ripe bananas | ½ banana | 5 bananas | 6 (1 spare) |
| **Cocoa & banana table:** cocoa powder | 1 tsp | ~30 g | pantry |
| **Berries & vanilla table:** frozen mixed berries, thawed | 3 tbsp (~40 g) | ~400 g | 1 bag (500 g) |
| **Berries & vanilla table:** vanilla extract (optional) | a drop | — | pantry |
| **Mango & coconut table:** frozen mango chunks, thawed | 3 tbsp (~40 g) | ~400 g | 1 bag (500 g) |
| **Mango & coconut table:** desiccated coconut | 1 tsp | ~30 g | 1 small bag |
| Maple syrup (optional) | 1 tsp | ~150 ml | 1 small bottle |

**Combined totals to buy:** 1 kg chia · 6 L soy drink · 6 bananas · 500 g frozen berries · 500 g frozen mango · 1 small bag desiccated coconut · maple syrup (optional) · cocoa (pantry).

**Supplies:** 30 disposable containers with lids (~300 ml) · 30 spoons or forks · permanent markers and labels (name + flavour) · 3 measuring cups · 3 sets of measuring spoons · one or two fridge shelves · napkins · a spare container for the demo.

**Allergy note:** contains **soy** (the drink). If someone cannot have soy, use oat or almond drink (check nut allergies). Ask about coconut and about seeds too: they are rare but real allergens.

## 4. Activity — Breakfast Match (~10 min + 6 min share-back)

**Goal:** turn "chia pudding is healthy" into a decision: what does this breakfast need to be on **tomorrow's** kind of day?

**Materials:** printed **`futedu-chia-pudding-breakfast-match.pdf`**, one per table, and a pen. The sheet has six situation cards and a list of add-ons grouped as carbs, protein, and fats and extras.

**How it runs:**
1. **Explain (1 min):** "Your pudding is in the fridge. Tomorrow's day decides what it needs. For each situation, choose up to 3 add-ons and write one sentence why."
2. **Tables work (9 min):** divide the six situations among the players (about 2 each at a 10-player table) and then compare answers as a table. The facilitator circulates and asks "what is missing?".
3. **Share-back (6 min):** each table reads out **one** situation and its answer; the other tables may disagree. Use the key to add one comment.
4. **Wrap-up line.**

**Facilitator key (suggestions, not strict answers; accept any reasoned answer):**

| Situation | What to listen for |
|---|---|
| 1 · Match at 10:00 | Carbs that digest easily: banana, mango or a spoon of oats. Not too much fat or fibre right before. |
| 2 · Rest day | The pudding as it is is fine; protein (soy yogurt) helps keep you full through school. No need for extra carbs. |
| 3 · Double session | Carbs plus protein: banana or oats, plus yogurt or peanut butter. Refuelling between sessions matters. |
| 4 · Early training, 7:00 | Easy, quick carbs: banana, dates, mango. Keep fats and extra fibre low. |
| 5 · Strength day | Protein and enough carbs: yogurt or peanut butter plus banana or oats. |
| 6 · Away game, bus at 8:00 | Something that eats cleanly on the bus: carbs first (banana, oats), keep it simple and familiar. |

**Wrap-up line:** *"Chia is great for fibre and omega-3 fats, but it is not a quick-carb food. Add the carbs when the day asks for them."*
