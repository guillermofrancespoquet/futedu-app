## 1. Goals

- **Gratitude (main value of the program):** practice thankfulness in a real shared task, from the opening round and the card missions to the thanks said before lunch.
- **Teamwork and integration:** trial and academy players work as one team.
- **Creativity:** each team invents its own original dish.
- **Responsible shopping:** plan, budget and buy sustainably, without wasting food.
- **Cooking and sharing:** cook and eat together.

**Where gratitude appears in the plan:** gratitude round (Wed) · gratitude mission on every Harvest Families card · "gratitude & inclusion ambassador" role · reflection on food waste · gratitude warm-up and thanks before eating (Thu) · thank-you notes · the 25-point "Gratitude & teamwork" criterion.

---

## 2. The Recipe Challenge (each team invents its own)

Each team creates **one original Thanksgiving dish**. Rules:

1. **1 source of quality protein** (legumes, tofu, chicken, turkey, eggs, fish).
2. **1 complex carbohydrate for sports recovery** (rice, potato, sweet potato, whole-wheat pasta, couscous).
3. **At least 2 vegetables or fresh fruits.**
4. **Zero ultra-processed foods and no industrial sauces.**

**Quick-cook note for the players:** the dish must be ready within the time limit, so use **pre-cooked or easy sources**: canned or jarred legumes (rinsed), plain pre-cooked rice or couscous, boiled eggs, plain pre-cooked chicken or turkey, tofu, tinned fish, frozen vegetables, pre-boiled potatoes or sweet potatoes. They must be **plain** (no sauces or added flavourings; short ingredient list on the label, so they still respect rule 4). Cut everything small to cook faster.

Be creative and original. Nutrition **checks and approves each recipe on Wednesday** (signature box in the Team Pack, including "fits the clock").

**Quantities:** each team cooks for its own members plus small tasting portions for the staff/judges.

*It is a tight hour: keep each block strict, and have the shopping logistics decided by staff in advance.*

### 1. Group game — "Harvest Families" (12 min)
Goal: form **5 mixed teams** in a fun way, with trial players evenly spread, and start the teamwork and gratitude work.

**Staff prep:** print the cards (`harvest-families-cards.pdf`: 5 families of 8 cards, one family per page) and fill the staff sheet (last page): write each player's name under a family, mark trial players with (T), aim for about 2 per family and a mix of ages, positions and friend groups. Hand cards **face-down** so nobody knows who is new. If the head-count is not 40, remove cards evenly from each family.

**Game:**
1. **Round 1 — Silent harvest (6 min):** everyone looks at their card and must find the others of their family using **only gestures and ingredient sounds**: no words, no showing the card, no writing.
2. **Round 2 — Line up (3 min):** the team lines up in silence by **birthday month**.
3. **Round 3 — Identity (2 min):** handshake or cheer and team name (the family name can be the base).
- **Debrief (1 min):** *How did you find your people without words? Who helped?*
- **Gratitude mission:** each card has a short personal mission (e.g. "thank a teammate who helped you today"). It is **not scored, but every player must complete it during the activity** (Wednesday or Thursday). The psychologist explains it in the welcome ("read the line at the bottom of your card: it is your gratitude challenge") and asks at the Thursday gratitude closing who completed theirs and how. It is also a line in the Thursday checklist of the Team Pack.

### 2. Gratitude opening in teams (8 min)
*"Name one person who has helped you get to where you are in football, and why."* Link to Thanksgiving and the values program: gratitude is a team skill.

### 3. Present the activity (7 min)
What happens Thursday, how the two days connect, the 4 recipe rules and the quick-cook note, how scoring works (Section 6). Message: *this is practice for working as a team and for being grateful.*

### 4. Teams invent their recipe (12 min)
Using page 2 of the Team Pack. Each team decides dish, idea and ingredients with quantities for its size. **Nutrition checks each recipe** against the 4 rules and the clock, and signs the box.

### 5. Budget + where to buy (8 min)
- **Budget per team:** [TO CONFIRM, still to negotiate], written on page 4 of the Team Pack.
- Teams choose **Mercadona or Consum**, check prices on the website/app and fit the list to the budget.
- **Shopping is on Thursday morning:** each team goes **together, as a team**, to buy what it needs. Supervising adults accompany the teams.
- The **treasurer** keeps the total and the receipt.
- **Basics (oil, salt, spices, etc.) are the teams' responsibility:** they buy them during the shopping and include them in their list and budget. The Team Pack reminds them on page 4 and in the Wednesday checklist.
- If they cannot find something, they adapt the recipe (keeping the 4 rules) and tell the staff.

### 6. Reflection: sustainable shopping, no food waste (6 min)
**Practical rules:** buy only what the recipe needs · check what the residence already has · prefer seasonal and loose products ("imperfect" fruit and veg are fine) · plan what to do with leftovers.
**Questions:**
- How can we be grateful for the food we have? How is gratitude connected to not wasting it?
- What will our team do to make sure nothing is thrown away?
Each team writes **one zero-waste commitment** (page 4).

### 7. Assign roles (5 min)
Teams sign up with a pen on page 3. **Everyone needs at least one role; in a team of 8, some roles are shared by two people.**

| Role | Job |
|---|---|
| **Team captain** | Keeps the plan and the time, makes sure everyone takes part |
| **Head chef** | Leads the cooking and follows the recipe |
| **Treasurer & shopper** | Budget, shopping list, receipt and who buys what |
| **Zero-waste & hygiene lead** | Quantities, leftovers, waste, clean hands and final clean-up |
| **Spokesperson & plating** | Presents the dish to the judges and decides how it looks on the plate |
| **Gratitude & inclusion ambassador** | Leads the thank-yous and makes sure everyone feels part of the team |

*Tip:* make sure trial players get a role with a clear task (not just helping).

### 8. Closing (2 min)
Quick round: *"One thing I'm grateful for from today."* Recap of Thursday: meeting time and place for the morning shopping, what to bring, time of the lunch session.

### The two rules (also written on page 5 of the Team Pack)

**Rule 1 · The Uneven Pantry (sharing resources).** Tools and basics are spread unfairly across the tables: one table has all the knives and boards, another the oil and spices, another the pans and bowls. Nobody has everything they need. To cook, teams must **ask with respect, negotiate turns and lend their own material**.

**Rule 2 · One Clock and the Final Rescue (time limit).** **35 minutes to cook** + **10 minutes of "Rescue Time"**. When the alarm rings at minute 35, teams that have finished and cleaned their area may **not sit down or present**: they must go to the tables of the teams that are behind and help them cut, plate and wash up. **At minute 45 every dish must be on the table.** A dish that is not ready by then **stays out of the contest and is not scored**, but the team still takes part and eats with everyone.

*These two rules replace the earlier idea of a surprise twist at the midpoint, since rule 1 already changes how the team works from the start.*

### Suggested table set-up for Rule 1 (5 tables, one per team)
Each table holds **one type of resource** in abundance and nothing else:

| Table | Holds |
|---|---|
| A | All knives and cutting boards (an adult supervises this table for safety) |
| B | Oil, salt, spices and other basics |
| C | Pans and pots |
| D | Bowls, trays, spoons and spatulas |
| E | Peelers, graters, measuring tools, cloths and paper towels |

### Notes for the staff
- **Alarm** at minute 35 and a visible clock for everyone; call out minute 45.
- **Helpers in Rescue Time** wash their hands before touching another team's food, and knives stay supervised.
- The psychologist observes Rescue Time closely: who goes to help, who waits, how the help is received. Since an unfinished dish now only loses its score (not lunch for everyone), the incentive to help comes from gratitude and team spirit; the **Gratitude & teamwork** score rewards it.
- **Gratitude closing:** before eating, each team says thanks (to its team, the staff, the food and the teams that helped).
- **Teamwork Award:** voted by the players for a team other than their own.
- **Team self-evaluation:** page 6 of the Team Pack (symbolic; the real score comes from the judges).

## 5. Logistics & Safety

- **Hygiene:** hand washing; clean surfaces; raw meat/poultry handled separately.
- **Knives and hot surfaces:** adult supervision at every station; use safe knives where possible.
- **Staff:** nutrition lead, psychologist, + coaches as station supervisors (about 1 per team).
- **Shopping:** adults accompany the teams to Mercadona/Consum.
